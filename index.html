LOW WATER

THE HELION COMPACT

Hard-political science fiction. Ice, intent, and the crime already under the floor — written for readers who will pay to sit in the cold with it.

A Core technician who can hear a dying plant — and the Compact that taught him not to hear people.

On Hearth Station the meters stay green while the water dies. Cade Voss logs the truth, then edits the official language, because that is what useful men do. When a bypass he failed to name kills someone in the next compartment, exile arrives dressed as honor.

LOW WATER opens a seven-book cycle about discipline, scarce heat, and the crime that was taught as virtue. Book One is the reservation: the useful man, the dying plant, and the official language that stays cleaner than the water.
